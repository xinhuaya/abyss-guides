## Overview

This Cookie Policy explains how Abyss Guides may use cookies and similar technologies.

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. They can help a website remember settings, measure performance, protect against abuse, or support advertising and affiliate tracking.

## Types of Cookies We May Use

### Essential Cookies

These help the site function, protect against abuse, maintain security, or remember basic preferences.

### Analytics Cookies

Analytics cookies help us understand which pages are visited, which guides need improvement, and how visitors find the site.

### Advertising or Affiliate Cookies

If advertising or affiliate links are enabled, third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies to measure ad performance, track referrals, or provide relevant ads.

## Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect site features or third-party services.

## Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy as the site changes. The updated version will be posted on this page.
