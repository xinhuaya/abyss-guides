## Overview

Abyss Guides is an independent fan-made guide site for deep-sea survival and exploration games, starting with Subnautica 2. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the site.

## Information We May Collect

We may collect limited information in the following situations:

- **Usage information:** Pages viewed, referring pages, device type, browser type, approximate location, and similar analytics data.
- **Contact information:** If you email us, we may receive your email address and any information you choose to include.
- **Technical information:** IP address, browser details, and security logs may be processed by hosting, security, analytics, or infrastructure providers.

## How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Operate and improve the website.
- Understand which guides are useful.
- Fix broken pages, outdated information, or technical issues.
- Respond to messages and corrections.
- Protect the site from spam, abuse, and security problems.

## Cookies and Analytics

Abyss Guides may use cookies or similar technologies for essential site functionality, analytics, performance measurement, and advertising if those services are enabled. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

## Advertising and Affiliate Links

Abyss Guides may use advertising networks or affiliate links in the future. These third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies.

## Third-Party Services

The site may rely on third-party services for hosting, analytics, security, search indexing, email, advertising, or affiliate tracking. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

## Data Retention

We keep information only as long as reasonably needed for site operation, analytics, security, communication, or legal compliance.

## Children

Abyss Guides is a general guide site and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take reasonable steps to remove it.

## Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as the site changes. The updated version will be posted on this page.

## Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to [support@abyssguides.com](mailto:support@abyssguides.com).
