# Abyss Guides Content Playbook

This is the working rulebook for Subnautica 2 articles on Abyss Guides.

## Indexing Standard

- Write one clear search intent per page. A page should answer one player problem, such as finding Silver, making a Power Cell, choosing a first base spot, or understanding a patch change.
- Put the practical answer near the top, then add route notes, tables, warnings, FAQ, and internal links.
- Do not publish thin translated pages. A localized page should have real localized title, metadata, headings, body copy, and internal links before it goes into sitemap or hreflang.
- Add a visible last-updated date on Early Access content.
- Use current official sources or direct gameplay verification for recipes, resource routes, patches, platforms, co-op details, and roadmap claims.
- Do not copy official screenshots, logos, creature renders, or wiki images. Use original Abyss Guides art, diagrams, tables, route cards, and checklists.

## Article Shape

Every publishable guide should include:

- Quick answer: 2-5 sentences that solve the main query fast.
- Route or decision cards: short sections players can scan while playing.
- At least one table when the topic has items, materials, priorities, risks, recipes, or steps.
- Read-next links to nearby guide pages.
- FAQ only when questions are real player follow-ups, not filler.
- Source note with date checked.

## Voice

- Write like a player explaining a route to another player.
- Avoid generic hype, padded introductions, and broad claims.
- Keep English and Chinese versions natural in their own language. Do not translate sentence-by-sentence when it sounds stiff.
- Be honest when data is patch-sensitive. Use "reported", "current build", or "needs recheck" when needed.

## Localization

- English remains the default URL set.
- Localized pages are published only after they have real localized title, metadata, headings, body copy, and navigation text.
- Current localized entry pages: `/zh`, `/ru`, `/de`, `/fr`, `/pt-BR`, `/es-419`, `/ko`, `/ja`, plus each locale's `/games/subnautica-2` hub.
- Unfinished localized article routes stay out of sitemap and hreflang until the page is localized.
- First article translation queue: Chinese, Russian, German, French, Brazilian Portuguese, Latin American Spanish, Korean, Japanese.

## Visuals

- Use original Q-style deep-sea visuals, route diagrams, checklist cards, crafting tables, and resource matrix blocks.
- Do not make pages text-only.
- Keep visual elements useful: show sequence, priority, safety, material chain, or route decision.
